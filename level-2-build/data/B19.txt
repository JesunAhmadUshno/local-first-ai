Title: laundry care

Cold water washing protects dark fabrics from fading in laundry. A stain is treated promptly before the garment enters the wash. Wool jumpers are dried flat to keep their shape after washing. Sorting whites from colours prevents dye transfer between clothes piles. A mesh bag protects delicate lace items in the drum. Vinegar in the rinse softens towels without fabric softener residue.
